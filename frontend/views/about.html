About Tracksuit Couture

We believe in comfort and style. Our tracksuits are designed with the modern woman in mind. Whether you're hitting the gym, catching a flight, or running errands, our designs offer both luxury and practicality.

Our Story

Tracksuit Couture was born in 2017 in a small studio in Milan, where two best friends — a fashion designer and a fitness enthusiast — decided to merge their passions. Their vision was simple: to redefine casualwear by combining elegance with comfort. What started as a side project quickly became a movement among women who wanted to look effortlessly chic, even on their most relaxed days.

The first Tracksuit Couture collection was handmade from premium cotton blends, crafted for comfort yet tailored for a flattering silhouette. Word spread quickly through social media, and soon celebrities and influencers began wearing our signature pink sets, turning athleisure into a lifestyle statement.
